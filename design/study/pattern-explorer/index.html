Rules

  • Parts are placed square to the board (0°). Two parts never come closer than 0.25 mm body to body, tested on the real rectangles; a part body stays at least 0.30 mm inside the outline.
  • Dense pitch is the diagonal of the footprint grown by the clearance, so the rule holds in every direction: for 0603, hypot(1.6+0.25, 0.8+0.25) = 2.127 mm. Medium is 1.4× that, sparse 2×.
  • Every pattern passes a final clearance check. Any LED it removes is reported under the view (most patterns remove none; Poisson, Voronoi and Lissajous crossings can).
  • Halftone and Apollonian use a ladder of sizes: 1206, 0805, 0603, 0402, plus the selected footprint if it is not one of those.
  • CSV: one row per LED, ref,x_mm,y_mm,rot_deg,footprint,w_mm,h_mm, origin at the outline centre, +x right, +y down as in KiCad. Rows follow the chase order.
  • True size depends on the screen. Browsers draw 96 CSS px per inch, but many displays are not exactly that; check the 10 mm bar against a ruler.
