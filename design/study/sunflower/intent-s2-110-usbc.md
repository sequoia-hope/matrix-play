# Design intent: Sunflower `s2-110-usbc` (Rev S3C)

Phase 0 specification for `hw/sunflower/s2-110-usbc/`. Every item has a concrete number or an explicit **"don't care"**.
Parent design: `design/intent.md` (Round Pendant) and `design/study/sunflower/README.md`. Items marked **(open — user sign-off)** require user confirmation at Gate 0.

## Purpose

A low-cost 4-layer, 38.0 mm round wearable RGB LED display worn as a pendant or watch, built exclusively from parts in local JLCPCB PCBA stock with standard non-filled through vias (no POFV). The front carries 110 `XL-1615RGBC-RF` RGB LEDs arranged in a golden-angle Vogel spiral starting with `D1` at the board centre `(0, 0)`, plus a 12-o'clock top-rim ambient light sensor (`Q2`). All control, power, charging, motion sensing, bring-up test pads, and connectors sit on the back, backed by a 35 mm round `403535` LiPo cell plugged into a right-angle Molex PicoBlade 1.25 mm socket (`BT1`). It charges and takes firmware over a back-side USB-C receptacle (`J1`), runs animations from an `RP2350A`, and sleeps at `≤ 85 µA` typ (`≤ 135 µA` worst-case max) until woken by a side button press (`SW1`) or accelerometer motion (`U9`). Intended for personal use and small prototype runs.

## Open source or confidential

Open source (`CERN-OHL-P-2.0`), public GitHub repository `https://github.com/sequoia-hope/round-pendant`, KiCad 9, Boardroom project layout (inherited from `design/intent.md`). Front display carries no text; board revision/repo identity on back silkscreen is **don't care** where displaced by functional connector/test-pad labels (tracked via schematic/PCB title block and `BOARD_ID = 6` hardware strap; **(open — user sign-off)**).

- **Sequencing & backfeed:** `AW20198QNR` digital inputs have abs max `-0.3 V to VCC` (`aw20198qnr.pdf` p. 6; `AD0/AD1` on `U2` tie directly to `VSYS = VCC`). ## Performance targets

- **System & QSPI clock:** `RP2350A` `150 MHz` max (`48–150 MHz` active), `QSPI` `≤ 75 MHz` (`13.3 ns` period).
- **Display refresh & PWM:** `≥ 60 fps` frame updates over `1 MHz` `I2C0`; `AW20198` `62.5 kHz` PWM / `5.05 kHz` 11-row scan (`SSCR.SSE = 1` spread-spectrum available if MLCC acoustic singing is audible).
- **ADC accuracy:** `VBAT` (`ADC0`) `±5%` uncalibrated (`±1.5%` LDO + `±1%` divider + `RP2350` `9.2 ENOB`), `±1.5%` after 1-point firmware trim; `LIGHT` (`ADC1`) monotonic `8-bit` indoor dimming bucket (**don't care** on absolute lux accuracy).
- **Wake latency & runtime:** `< 50 ms` button/motion wake (**don't care** below `100 ms`); `≥ 2 h` typical animated runtime on `400 mAh` cell, `≥ 4 months` shelf sleep at `~73 µA`.

## Environment

- **Temperature:** `0–35 °C` operating ambient (`-20 to +60 °C` storage without LiPo; `0–45 °C` charge / `-10 to +60 °C` discharge with LiPo). Moisture/vibration/enclosure airflow: **don't care** (bare/open prototype in still air).
- **ESD:** `±8 kV` contact on `J1` USB-C via `U7` (`USBLC6-2P6`); buttons and back test pads: **don't care** beyond `1 kΩ` series limiting and bench handling.
- **Thermal:** Under firmware `ADC4` thermal management (`R19`: continuous `GCC ≤ 105` / `~0.68 W` on pouch-backed battery at `35 K/W` → `ΔT ≤ 23 K`, board `≤ 48 °C` at `25 °C` ambient; `GCC ≤ 9` CC / `≤ 24` CV gated on `~{PGOOD} == 0 || ~{CHG} == 0` while charging → board `≤ 45 °C`), all components remain well inside `SW1/SW2` (`+70 °C`), `J1` (`+80 °C`), `85 °C` IC/LED, `45 °C/60 °C` LiPo, and wearable skin-contact limits. Uncapped `300–354 mA` continuous full-white (`1.26–1.55 W`, `ΔT ≈ 55 K`) is prohibited except for `< 15 s` bench tests.

## Mechanical

- **Outline & mounting:** `38.0 mm` circle, `0` mounting holes; held by rim/bezel in a future housing (**don't care** for this prototype revision; bezel must notch `J1`, `SW1`, `SW2`, and leave an optical window at `Q2`).
- **Heights:** Front `≤ 0.95 mm` (`Q2` `0.80 ± 0.15 mm`, `D1`–`D110` `0.60 ± 0.10 mm`); PCB `1.60 ± 0.16 mm`; Back components `≤ 3.40 mm` (`BT1` `3.20 +0.20/−0.05 mm` unmated / `3.40 mm` mated; `J1` `3.26 ± 0.10 mm`).
- **Battery stack & harness:** The `35 mm × 4.0 mm` `403535` cell sits behind `B.Cu` (`~9.6 mm` total bare stack). Because `BT1` `(−11.40, −10.20 mm, −110°)` faces inward toward `U3` (`4.04 mm` to `U3` body, `3.02 mm` to `C9`), the `1.25 mm` plug must be inserted **before** bonding the cell over a **`≥ 2.4 mm` insulating foam/carrier standoff + `0.05 mm` Kapton tape** over exposed `B.Cu` test pads (**open — user sign-off**).
- **Connector overhangs:** `J1` mouth overhangs south edge by `+0.25–0.30 mm` on axis; `SW1`/`SW2` plungers overhang NE/NW rim by `+0.40 mm` nominal unpressed (`+0.20 mm` pressed; `0.00 to −0.20 mm` worst-case tolerance — **open — user sign-off**).

## Manufacturing

- **Fab & PCBA:** JLCPCB 4-layer standard FR-4 (`JLC04161H-7628`, `1.6 mm`, `ENIG 1U"` recommended over HASL for fine-pitch QFN/LGA/LED coplanarity), double-sided PCBA with **`B.Cu` reflowed in Pass 1 and `F.Cu` (`Q2` + `110` MSL-3 LEDs) reflowed in Pass 2 at `≤ 245 °C` peak** so `Q2` undergoes **only 1 reflow pass** (`xyc-pt19c-l1.pdf` p. 11), and **no `F.Cu` wave/hand soldering on `J1`'s 4 PTH shield slots** (`0.18–0.29 mm` from `D10/D104/D110`; tack `J1` outer `B.Cu` slot edges only on `Rev S3C`). Prototype quantity `5–10` boards (`$12.55/board` parts BOM at qty 10, meeting `< $15/board` parts target; PCBA setup/feeder fees **don't care** for prototype).
- **Hand assembly:** Plugging in `BT1` harness and applying Kapton + `2.4 mm` foam standoff + `403535` LiPo cell after bench bring-up.

## Serviceability

- Hobby / prototype wearable, not field-repairable. `14` `B.Cu` test pads (`R9`) cover all rails, SWD, I2C0, UART1, and spare ADC (accessible before LiPo bonding). Major `B.Cu` ICs/connectors carry silkscreen callouts where space permits, supplemented by `bringup-map-bottom.png` and `pcbview`.

## Compliance

- None formal (personal / prototype wearable, no radio).

## Known risks and open questions for user sign-off

1. **`BT1` inward-mating corridor & `2.4 mm` battery spacer (open — user sign-off):** `BT1` at `(−11.40, −10.20 mm, −110°)` points inward with `3.02–4.04 mm` clearance to `C9`/`U3`; plug must be pre-mated with upward-angled harness entry before installing Kapton + `≥ 2.4 mm` rear foam/carrier spacer + `403535` cell (`~9.6 mm` stack).
2. **`403535` cell charge rate (open — user sign-off):** `R4 = 3.0 kΩ ±1%` sets `263–328 mA` (`297 mA` typ, `~0.58–0.82C` on `400–450 mAh`). Confirm cell datasheet permits `≥ 0.8C` charge or swap `R4` to `4.7 kΩ` (`189 mA`).
3. **Active `ADC4` thermal cap, QSPI XIP mode & `SW1/SW2` rim-overhang tolerance (open — user sign-off):** Confirm firmware thermal caps (`GCC ≤ 105` pouch-backed wearable battery at `25 °C` amb; `GCC ≤ 9` CC charge / `≤ 24` CV taper gated on `PGOOD == 0 || CHG == 0` for `45 °C` LiPo limit), 1-bit/2-bit SPI XIP (`0x0B`/`0xBB`) fallback on `Rev S3C` (`U4.8` `8.56 mm` decoupling SSO margin), and `EVQP7J01P` `+0.40 mm` nominal (`0.00 to −0.20 mm` worst-case tolerance) fingernail rim actuation.
4. **Back silkscreen identity omission & variant firmware deltas (open — user sign-off):** Accept omitting board name/rev/URL on `B.SilkS` (`BOARD_ID = 6` via `NT1` on `GPIO19`), `VBAT_EN` on `GPIO28`, `UART1` on `GPIO24/25`, `2× AW20198QNR` (`GCR = 0xB9`), and `≥ 30 ms` `Q1` ADC settle.
5. **Low-`VBAT` green/blue headroom:** At `13.33 mA` peak, `XL-1615` `G/B` `VF` (`2.9 V` typ / `3.4 V` max bin) + `AW20198` `SW` drop (`208 mV` typ at `200 mA`) + `CS` headroom (`250 mV`) ≈ `3.36 V` typ (`3.86 V` max bin); high-`VF` `G/B` bins compress gracefully near `3.55 V` cutoff.
