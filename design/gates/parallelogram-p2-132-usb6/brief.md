## 1. Board outline and size
Skewed parallelogram, enclosure-free bare wearable pendant outline, unchanged from `p2-132-usb`:
- Width: `25.00 mm` (`x ∈ [-12.50, +12.50] mm` relative to board center `(100.0, 100.0) mm`).
- Side height: `62.00 mm`; skew: `8.00 mm` (`tan(α) = 8/25 = 0.32`, slant angle `17.7447°`), giving `70.00 mm` unfilleted sharp tip-to-tip height and **`69.260 mm` filleted overall height** (`y ∈ [-34.630, +34.630] mm`).
- Corner fillets: **`R = 1.000 mm`** at all four corners (`NW` center `(-11.50, -33.63)`, `SW` `(-11.50, +26.27)`, `NE` `(+11.50, -26.27)`, `SE` `(+11.50, +33.63)`).
- Straight vertical edge spans:
  - **West edge (`x = -12.50 mm`):** `y ∈ [-33.630, +26.270] mm` (`59.900 mm` straight vertical run between corner fillet tangents).
  - **East edge (`x = +12.50 mm`):** `y ∈ [-26.270, +33.630] mm` (`59.900 mm` straight vertical run between corner fillet tangents).

### Why West edge (`x = -12.50 mm`) was chosen over East edge (`x = +12.50 mm`)
- **East edge (`x = +12.50 mm`, straight `y ∈ [-26.270, +33.630] mm`) fails at the South end:** from `y = +6.27 mm` down to `y = +33.94 mm`, the East perimeter is continuously occupied by `R9` (`[+6.27, +8.13]`), `Q1` (`[+6.61, +8.60]`), `R11` (`[+8.93, +9.87]`), `C6` (`[+10.19, +11.11]`), `U8` (`[+11.71, +13.49]`), `R8` (`[+13.68, +14.62]`), `BT1` (`[+20.20, +26.34]`), `R45` (`[+26.17, +28.03]`), and `SW4` (`[+27.16, +33.94]`). Zero `5.29 mm`-tall `EVQP7J01P` courtyard pocket exists on the East South end without moving inherited parts.
- **West edge (`x = -12.50 mm`, straight `y ∈ [-33.630, +26.270] mm`) has clean North and South pockets:**
  - **`SW5` (North, `y = -22.00 mm`):** Courtyard centerline `[-13.05, -9.31] × [-24.59, -19.41]` clears `SW3` courtyard (`y_max = -25.00`) by **`0.410 mm` (`0.650 mm` copper-to-copper)** and `SW2` courtyard by **`13.310 mm` (`13.500 mm` copper-to-copper)**. Copper pads span `x ∈ [-12.10, -9.60]`, giving **`0.400 mm` copper-to-edge clearance** (`>= 0.30 mm` JLC 4L routed rule). Peg-less SMD construction (`EVQP7J01P`, `0` new holes) preserves all `132` front LEDs untouched.
  - **`SW6` (South, `y = +22.80 mm`):** Courtyard sits `9.30 mm` south of the LiPo pocket (`y_max = +13.50`), clears `J1` rotated courtyard polygon by **`1.806 mm` true polygon distance** (`0.987 mm` axis-aligned stroke bbox, `2.258 mm` pad copper-to-copper), clears `U4` courtyard by **`2.910 mm` centerline (`2.810 mm` stroke bbox)**, and keeps its `3.50 mm` plastic body (`y_max = +24.550 mm`), `4.70 mm` terminal span (`y_max = +25.150 mm`), and `5.00 mm` copper pad (`y_max = +25.300 mm`) respectively **`1.720 mm`, `1.120 mm`, and `0.970 mm` north** of the SW `R = 1.000 mm` fillet tangent (`y = +26.270 mm`).
  - **3D LiPo pocket envelope (`x ∈ [-10.0, +10.0]`, `y ∈ [-21.5, +13.5] mm`):** All side switches (`SW1`, `SW2`, `SW5`) sit at `|x| = 10.850 mm`, so their `1.35 ± 0.20 mm` (`1.55 mm` max, `evqp7j01p.pdf` p. 2) plastic bodies reach inward to `|x| = 9.38–9.40 mm` (`18.76–18.80 mm` clear lateral span `x ∈ [-9.38, +9.38]`; `SW5` body spans `y ∈ [-23.75, -20.25]`, extending `1.25 mm` South and `0.62 mm` East into a `20 × 35 mm` box, just as `SW1`/`SW2` extend `0.62 mm` at mid-span). Therefore: (a) a **`≤ 18.0 mm`-wide cell** (e.g. `601835` `18 × 35 mm` or `501035` `10 × 35 mm`) clears `SW1`/`SW2`/`SW5` bodies laterally on `1.0 mm` foam tape; (b) a full `20 × 35 mm` (`602035`/`802035`/`102035`) cell requires **`≥ 1.8–2.0 mm` VHB/foam standoff** (`0.25–0.45 mm` Z-clearance above `1.55 mm` max `EVQP7J01P` housings; `U1`/`U2`/`U3` QFNs are `0.75–0.85 mm` and `R46`/`C71` `0402`s at `x = -7.40 mm` are `0.50 mm`).

## 5. Layer count & stackup lock
Locked to **4 layers, `1.6 mm` nominal (`1.5862 mm` bare copper-dielectric) FR-4, JLCPCB `JLC04161H-7628`, `ENIG 1U"`, `POFV = Yes` (IPC-4761 Type VII epoxy-filled & copper-capped via-in-pad, VIPPO)** (`1 oz` `35 µm` outer / `0.5 oz` `15.2 µm` inner, `0.2104 mm` `7628` prepreg `Er = 4.4`, `1.065 mm` core, `0.30/0.15 mm` through vias, `0` blind/buried vias; **`POFV = Yes` is mandatory** because copper-capping plates all `164` via-in-paste sites (`89` `F.Paste` + `75` `B.Paste`, comprising `124` non-GND SMD via-in-pads plus `GND` SMD pad vias) completely flat *before* stencil solder-paste printing — preventing any solder wicking on `U6`, `J1`, `U7`, `U9`, or `D1..D132` and eliminating `F.SilkS` web dimples — while exposed thermal pads `U3=5`, `U5=2`, `U1=5`, `U2=6` contain `18` `GND` thermal vias):
- `F.Cu` (L1): LED pads, `Q2`, `CS` vertical column buses, inter-column routing alleys.
- `In1.Cu` (L2): **Dedicated whole-board solid `GND` reference plane** (`plane GND`, `1` unbroken piece; cut only around `VREG_LX` switch copper `LX cutout L2` per `decisions.md` 2026-11-07).
- `In2.Cu` (L3): Horizontal `SW` scan buses, power (`VSYS`, `+3V3`, `+1V1` `COUT`/`DVDD` spokes), MCU GPIO / I2C escape tracks, with `LX cutout L3` blocking traces directly `0.21 mm` above `B.Cu` `VREG_LX`/`L1.2`.
- `B.Cu` (L4): All ICs, passives, buttons, `J1`, `BT1`, `1` whole-piece RP2350 `+1V1` ring (`RP2350 ring`) and `1` whole-piece `+3V3` band (`RP2350 band`), `0`-via `12 MHz` crystal, and short USB/QSPI links.

## 6. Density philosophy
Compact wearable (`0402` passives for logic/RC/I2C/LED-driver decoupling, `0603`/`0805` for bulk power capacitance and `2016` for `L1`). Decorative skewed diamond lattice web on `F.SilkS` (clipped clear of all `F.Mask` pads) + selective functional/connector/button/identity labels and major IC designators on `B.SilkS`; `F.Fab`/`B.Fab` assembly drawings carry full passive designators for bench rework.
