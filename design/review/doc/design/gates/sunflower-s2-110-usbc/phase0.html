Independent Review (2026-11-08, fresh-context agy reviewer)

Reviewer inspected only intent-s2-110-usbc.md, intent-and-brief.md, and design/datasheets/*.pdf. Independently re-derived and confirmed: IOUT,max = 13.33 mA, 11-SW duty = 0.08049 (15.9375 / 198 = 255 / 3168), ILED,avg = 1.073 mA/die, full-white = 354.2 mA LED typ (357.8 mA w/ 2× IACT_VCC), 400.0 mA combined scan-slot peak, 1 MHz I2C0 frame 3.63–3.84 ms (~22–23% @ 60 fps), ICHG = 296.7 mA (263.0–328.3 mA), and ~68–73 µA de-duplicated typ sleep current.
