Scope & Inherited Earlier Artifacts

parallelogram-p2-132-usb6 (hw/parallelogram/p2-132-usb6/) diverges from its parent parallelogram-p2-132-usb (hw/parallelogram/p2-132-usb/) at Phase 4 in two clean, user-directed mechanical/layout ways while keeping all active ICs (U1..U9, L1, Y1, Q1, Q2), J1 ((-1.24, +26.82), rot = 162.2553°), SW1..SW3, and all 132 front LEDs (D1..D132) 100% untouched: 1. West long-edge buttons (SW5, SW6): Adds two peg-less side-actuated Panasonic EVQP7J01P (C2828254) tactile buttons (SW5 North USER_C, SW6 South USER_D) on the 62.0 mm West vertical edge, wired active-low to GND with internal RP2350 pull-ups, 1 kΩ series protection resistors (R46, R47), and 100 nF debounce capacitors (C71, C72). 2. SE wing battery & switch clearance ("Option 1 — move the switch"): Rotates right-angle battery receptacle BT1 (CJT A1251WR-S-2P C225111) 90° CCW from (+8.40, +20.05, +90.0°) to (+7.97, +23.00, 0.0°) so its mating mouth (y = +20.70) faces straight North (-Y) toward board center / LiPo pocket with 6.03 mm unobstructed plug-entry runway to 0402 R8 (7.20 mm to U8), eliminating the old 1.28 mm tilted-plug gap to U5, while shifting SE wing button SW4 (XKB TS-1187A-B-A-B C318884, USER_B) deeper into the SE corner at (+8.15, +30.55), rot = 162.2553° (bezel-aligned parallel to J1 and the 17.7447° South slanted bezel) and relocating three 0402 passives (C1, R5, R45) + nudged bare test pads TP1/TP3.
