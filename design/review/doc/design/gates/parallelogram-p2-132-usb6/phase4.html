Scope & Inherited Earlier Artifacts

parallelogram-p2-132-usb6 (hw/parallelogram/p2-132-usb6/) diverges from its parent parallelogram-p2-132-usb (hw/parallelogram/p2-132-usb/) at Phase 4 by adding two peg-less side-actuated Panasonic EVQP7J01P (C2828254) tactile buttons (SW5 North USER_C, SW6 South USER_D) on one 62.0 mm vertical long edge, wired active-low to GND with internal RP2350 pull-ups, 1 kΩ series protection resistors (R46, R47), and 100 nF debounce capacitors (C71, C72). Everything else on p2-132-usb — including J1's slanted position (-1.24, +26.82) and rotation 162.2553° (usb_s = -2.45, usb_ye = 3.60) — stays unchanged.

Earlier phases inherited from parent & verified for the delta: - Phase 0 (Intent): design/intent.md & design/study/parallelogram/README.md §5 (parallelogram family divergences logged in design/decisions.md). - Phase 1 (Datasheets & Parts): design/parts/{rp2350-core.md,buttons.md,parallelogram-io.md,passives.md} and design/datasheets/evqp7j01p.pdf (EVQP7J01P: 3.50 × 2.90 × 1.35 mm SMD side-actuated without boss/pegs, 1.70 mm knob, push travel 0.20 mm, rating 10 µA 2 V DC to 50 mA 12 V DC, max ON/OFF bounce 10 ms). - Phase 2 (Schematic): hw/parallelogram/p2-132-usb6/parallelogram.kicad_sch (mcu.kicad_sch adds SW5/R46/C71 -> /MCU/USER_C on U3.13 GPIO9 and SW6/R47/C72 -> /MCU/USER_D on U3.2 GPIO0; kicad-cli sch erc --severity-all = 0 violations across all 5 sheets). - Phase 3 (Net classification): design/nets-parallelogram-p2-132-usb6.csv (118 rows: 114 parent rows + /MCU/USER_C, /MCU/USER_D, Net-(R46-Pad2), Net-(R47-Pad2) in SIGNAL 3.4 V, 0.10 mm clearance).
