1. Feasibility Study & Mechanical Verification Output

Why SSE (23.95° East of South, 8.35° Non-Radial Skew) Instead of Due South (0°)

At due South (100.0, 113.5, 180°), TYPE-C-31-M-22's two 5.78 mm-spaced Ø0.65 mm (r = 0.325 mm) NPTH locating holes collide with Variant F's fixed golden-angle LED pads (< 0.08 mm clearance). Sweeping (x, y, θ) along the southern rim found an exact zero-LED-move window at (107.787, 110.926) mm, KiCad rot = -156.05° (203.95°) (23.95° E of S, an 8.35° non-radial skew from the 32.30° radial normal at mouth center (109.865, 115.605) mm): - NPTH #1 (109.953, 108.684) mm lands inside the {D234, D235, D231, D226} inter-LED gap with 0.220 mm hole-edge-to-pad-edge clearance (D234.4), 0.304 mm on In3.Cu (CS5_16), and ≥ 0.201 mm across all 8 copper layers. - NPTH #2 (104.671, 111.030) mm lands inside the {D76, D66, D74, D64} inter-LED gap with 0.220 mm hole-edge-to-pad-edge clearance (D74.3), 0.214 mm to nearest F.Cu track (CS2_12), and ≥ 0.201 mm across all inner/bottom layers. - Local rip-up bounding box: Primary southern crescent X ∈ [91.3, 114.9] mm, Y ∈ [105.0, 118.8] mm (~195 mm², 17% of the 1,134 mm² disc, with minor route adjustments reaching Y = 93.29 mm; 85.6% [5,261 / 6,144] of Variant F's copper items preserved verbatim).

Datasheet vs Audit Verification (type-c-31-m-22.pdf p. 1 vs parallelogram-io.md §1)

  1. Peg diameter & length on 1.0 mm PCB: Datasheet p. 1 (Front View & SECTION A-A) specifies 2-Ø0.50 mm pegs (5.78 mm span, matching parallelogram-io.md §1 and STEP) of 0.70 ± 0.05 mm length (0.65–0.75 mm), terminating 0.30 mm nominal (0.15 mm worst-case min board / max peg) below F.Cu on our 1.00 mm ± 10% PCB (guaranteed zero front protrusion).
  2. 4-SMD-pad shell hold-down (2.30 × 1.80 mm each = 16.56 mm² B.Cu area) & signal-row land note: Pure axial mating shear capacity (~330–500 N) is > 16× the 5–20 N rated insertion/extraction force, while out-of-plane prying is foil-peel-limited (~35–55 N / ~0.15–0.22 N·m). Through-hole TYPE-C-31-M-12 cannot be used because its 1.28 mm PTH legs would protrude 0.28 mm through the 1.0 mm front LED array and destroy front LED sites. As logged in design/decisions.md (2026-11-08), S1 retains parallelogram's verified 2.30 × 1.80 mm pads (92% of datasheet 2.50 × 1.80 mm, +0.27 mm heel/toe fillet beyond the 1.48 mm gull-wing foot) because widening S1 to 2.50 × 1.80 mm reduces hole_clearance to 6 fixed front-LED escape vias (CS5_17, CS5_16, SW2_3, CS2_18, SW2_6, CS2_10) to 0.105–0.138 mm (< 0.200 mm DRC rule); J1's 12 rear signal pads sit at y_rel = +2.300 mm (1.14 mm long vs parallelogram-io.md's y = 2.240 mm, 1.15 mm long), a benign +0.055–0.060 mm outboard shift over 0.40 mm-protruding tails that widens B.Cu NPTH clearance to 0.247 mm.
