Bench Bring-Up & Smoke-Test Plan — Sunflower s2-110-usbc-s3d (Rev S3D)

Board: sunflower.kicad_pcb (Ø38.0 mm, 4-layer In2.Cu GND, ENIG, 110× XL-1615RGBC-RF + 2× AW20198QNR + RP2350A + full-SMT TYPE-C-31-M-22). Parent plan: bringup-s2-110-usbc.md (Rev S3C). Probe map: bringup-map-bottom.png and assembly-bottom.png.

[!IMPORTANT] Perform all Steps 1–7 BEFORE bonding a 403535 LiPo pouch to B.Cu. Thirteen of the 14 bare Ø1.0 mm test pads lie inside the Ø35 mm battery envelope; once bring-up passes, apply 0.05 mm Kapton tape over B.Cu exposed test pads and provide a ≥ 2.4 mm insulating foam/carrier standoff so the pouch clears J1 (3.26 mm) and mated BT1 (3.40 mm).

What Rev S3D Simplifies vs Rev S3C During Bring-Up

  1. Zero manual soldering on J1: J1 (TYPE-C-31-M-22, C530612) has four gull-wing SMD shell pads reflowed automatically in Pass 1 (B.Cu) with 0 PTH slots near F.Cu LEDs — no microscope hand-tacking is required before plugging in USB-C.
  2. Unoccluded B.SilkS polarity & net labels: BT1's "+", "-", and "BATT 3.7V" markers and J1's "USB 5V" marker sit clearly outside the assembled connector housings, and Molex's restricted housing area + 0.20 mm BATTERY clearance eliminate Rev S3C's 0.107 mm VBAT-to-BT1.2 via hazard.
  3. Full 4-bit Quad-SPI (0xEB) XIP at up to 75 MHz: Moving C22 (100 nF) right beside U4.8 (1.40 mm pad-to-pad over 0.2104 mm In2.Cu GND) eliminates Rev S3C's 1x/2x SPI XIP fallback.
  4. Hardware Board ID = 4 (0b100): NT1 (GPIO19 → GND) and NT2 (GPIO20 → GND) strap s2-110-usbc-s3d to ID = 4 (S3C = 6, pn-dense = 5, pn-dense-1d = 3).

2. Unpowered Visual & Ohmmeter Inspection (Step 1)

  1. Microscope visual check (B.Cu & F.Cu): - Confirm J1 (TYPE-C-31-M-22) overhangs the South rim by ~0.33 mm with clean fillets on all 12 rear signal/power pads and all 4 B.Cu gull-wing SMD shell tabs. - Confirm BT1 (NW rim) has clean fillets and visible "+" (North/NE pad 1 = VBAT) and "-" (South/SW pad 2 = /Power/BAT_N) silkscreen markers outside its body. - Confirm L1 (3.3 µH) polarity mark faces West (+1V1 / C18 side, pad 1), and all QFNs (U1, U2, U3, U5) sit flat on their paste-free thermal-via pads.
  2. Cold continuity & resistance checks (< 0.5 V DMM resistance mode on Ø1.0 mm test pads & 0402 pads only — never probe 0.40 mm-pitch QFN pins): - Verify no rail shorts between TP8/J1-shell (GND) and TP13 (VBUS), TP3 (VBAT), TP1 (VSYS), TP2 (+3V3), and TP4 (+1V1). - Verify BT1.1 ↔ BT1.2 resistance is > 1 MΩ. - Verify USB-C Rd pull-downs: R1 = 5.1 kΩ ±1% and R2 = 5.1 kΩ ±1% to GND.

3. Current-Limited USB-C First Power-Up (No Battery Attached, Steps 2–4)

  1. Step 2 — Current-limited 5.0 V / 100 mA apply via USB-C breakout: Expect 12–28 mA in blank-flash ROM USB boot (U5 cold-starts in USB100 until +3V3 enables EN1).
  2. Step 3 — Rail & clock verification: - TP13 (VBUS) = 4.90–5.05 V - TP1 (VSYS) = 4.30–4.50 V (4.40 V typ) - TP2 (+3V3) = 3.250–3.350 V (< 10 mVpp ripple) - TP4 (+1V1) = 1.067–1.133 V (< 20 mVpp ripple) - C24 pad 1 (10× < 10 pF probe): 12.000 MHz oscillation (~0.8–1.8 Vpp).
  3. Step 4 — USB Full-Speed enumeration & SW2 (BOOTSEL): Verify 2e8a:000f Raspberry Pi RP2350 Boot on host PC, and confirm holding SW2 ("BOOT") while pulsing TP5 (RUN) low re-enters USB boot mode.

4. Firmware Smoke-Test Sequence & Mandatory Register Rules (Steps 5–6)

[!CAUTION] Mandatory firmware rules on s2-110-usbc-s3d: 1. GPIO4 / GPIO5 (I2C0) pad drive = 12 mA BEFORE first AW20198 write (VOL ≤ 0.14 V across 1 kΩ R21/R22, meeting POR LGC = 0 VIL ≤ 0.40 V). 2. AW20198 GCR (0x00, Page 0) = 0xB9 (LGC = 1 bit 3 for 3.3 V thresholds, SWSEL = 1011 for SW1..SW11, CHIPEN = 1). 3. ADC & sleep pad hygiene: Set OD = 1, IE = 0, PDE = 0, PUE = 0 on GPIO26 (ADC0) and GPIO27 (ADC1), clear PDE = 0, PUE = 0, IE = 0 on unused/strapped GPIOs (GPIO0, 7..9, 14..23, erratum RP2350-E9), drive GPIO6 (LED_SDB) actively low/high, and wait ≥ 30 ms (6 τ) after asserting GPIO28 = 1 (VBAT_EN) before sampling ADC0. 4. Active ADC4 thermal cap: Cap continuous full-white GCC ≤ 105 on pouch-backed battery (R_th ≈ 35 K/W → ≤ 48 °C skin / < 60 °C LiPo / < 70 °C EVQP7J01P) and GCC ≤ 9 CC (0 at low-bat CC, ≤ 24 CV taper) whenever (GPIO13 ~{PGOOD} == 0) || (GPIO12 ~{CHG} == 0) (≤ 45 °C LiPo charge limit with fixed 10 kΩ TS). (Note: 4-bit Quad-SPI 0xEB XIP up to 75 MHz is fully supported in hardware on Rev S3D thanks to C22 at U4.8.)

Smoke-Test Checklist

  1. Board ID (GPIO19..21 with internal pull-ups enabled at boot): Read (GPIO21<<2)|(GPIO20<<1)|GPIO19 → expect 4 (0b100), then disable pull-ups and clear IE = 0 on GPIO19..21.
  2. I2C1 (GPIO2/3, 400 kHz) accelerometer (U9 LIS2DW12 at 0x19): Read WHO_AM_I (0x0F) → 0x44.
  3. I2C0 (GPIO4/5, 1 MHz; GPIO6 LED_SDB = 1, wait 2 ms): Confirm ACK at 0x20 (U1) and 0x25 (U2), write GCR = 0xB9, GCC = 0x40, and walk R/G/B across D1..D110 (330 dies).
  4. Button SW1 ("USER", GPIO11) & light sensor Q2 (GPIO1 → GPIO27/ADC1, 15 ms settle): Confirm active-low press on SW1 and dark < 0.15 V vs lamp 1.0–2.9 V response on ADC1.

5. LiPo Battery Attach, XB9901A Wake & Deep-Sleep Check (Step 7)

  1. Verify plug polarity against B.SilkS "+" / "-" markers: Red (+) to BT1 Pin 1 ("+", North/NE pad); Black (-) to BT1 Pin 2 ("-", South/SW pad).
  2. First-attach XB9901A (U8) wake: Attach USB-C (J1) briefly (1–2 s) if TP3 initially reads 0.0 V on first cell mate; verify TP3 holds VBAT (3.7–4.2 V) after USB unplug.
  3. Charge current (263–328 mA via R4 = 3.0 kΩ) & ADC0 telemetry (±50 mV vs TP3): Verify on USB 5.0 V / 500 mA with LED_SDB = 0.
  4. Deep-sleep current (intent R17): Feed 3.70 V via µA meter / PPK2 into BT1, wake U8, unplug USB-C, enter dormant sleep → confirm ≤ 85 µA typ (68–85 µA expected, ≤ 135 µA max).
