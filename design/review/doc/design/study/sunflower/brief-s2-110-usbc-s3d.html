Front LED Spiral Keepout Decision (Rev S3C → Rev S3D)

Removing TYPE-C-31-M-12's four PTH shield slots freed the four outer South-rim slot sites (±4.32, +12.47 / +16.65), while TYPE-C-31-M-22 retains the exact same two Ø0.65 mm NPTH locating-peg holes at (±2.89, +13.00 mm). Testing pure un-steered Vogel positions vs keeping S3C's South-rim LED relaxation showed that un-pushed spiral seeds place D7 / D107 1.6×1.5 mm SMD pads within < 0.12 mm of the (±2.89, +13.00) NPTH drills (violating the 0.20 mm hole_clearance rule), whereas keeping the existing South-rim LED positions (r = 1.951 mm min / 2.688 mm mean spacing) preserves 0.317 mm (D7.4) and 0.585 mm (D107.2) NPTH-to-copper clearance, keeps D1..D110 coordinates identical to Rev S3C firmware LED lookup tables, and avoids re-crowding South-rim F.Cu matrix channels. We therefore dropped the four obsolete PTH slot keepouts in s2-110-usbc-s3d's mechanical DRC/zones while preserving the S3C 110-LED coordinates (recorded in decisions.md).

4. Assembly sides & mandatory PCBA reflow instructions

  • 100%-SMT double-sided assembly at JLCPCB (0 through-hole soldering), requiring an explicit JLCPCB PCBA engineering instruction (since factories otherwise default to flat F.Cu first):
  • Pass 1 — Back (B.Cu): Reflow all 80 B.Cu SMD parts first (U1..U9, Q1, L1, Y1, full-SMT J1, BT1, SW1, SW2, 38 MLCCs, 26 resistors).
  • Pass 2 — Front (F.Cu, ≤ 245 °C peak, underside carrier support): Reflow 110 × XL-1615RGBC-RF (0.60 mm, MSL Level 3 168 h) and Q2 (XYC-PT19C-L1, 0.80 mm, 24-hour open-bag floor life / 65 °C × 24 h bake per xyc-pt19c-l1.pdf pp. 11, 13) in Pass 2 so Q2 sees only 1 reflow pass. Specify ≥ 3.5 mm underside carrier clearance for Pass 2 stencil printing and allow red SMD glue under J1 (~0.65 g over ~14.3 mm² ≈ 29 g/in²) if requested by JLCPCB CAM for inverted reflow retention.

5. Layer count and Rev S3D stackup role

  • 4 layers, 1.6 mm nominal thickness (JLC04161H-7628), ENIG finish, standard 0.30/0.15 mm (0.35/0.15 mm power) through vias, zero vias in SMD signal pads (no POFV).
  • F.Cu (L1, 1 oz): 110 RGB LEDs + Q2 pads; radial CS cathode triplets and outer SW scan arcs.
  • In1.Cu (L2, 0.5 oz): Inner routing layer (1.2906 mm above B.Cu, 0.2104 mm below F.Cu) — circumferential SW scan arcs, cross-wedge CS jumps, power trunks, and RP2350 +1V1 COUT (0.50 mm) / DVDD (0.40 mm) power spokes, guarded by LX inner guard so zero supply/analog copper crosses above VREG_LX/L1.
  • In2.Cu (L3, 0.5 oz): Unbroken solid GND plane (1 piece, 0.2104 mm directly beneath B.Cu ICs), perforated only by via antipads, J1's 2 NPTH holes, and LX cutout L3 directly under U3.48/L1 (see quantitative justification in stackup-4l-s3d.md §1).
  • B.Cu (L4, 1 oz): All active ICs (U1..U9), J1, BT1, SW1/2, passives, test pads, 1-piece RP2350 ring (+1V1) and 1-piece RP2350 band (+3V3), and solid GND EPs (17 paste-free thermal vias).

6. Density & probing philosophy

  • 0402 standard passives, 0603 only for 10 µF / 22 µF bulk MLCCs and 0806 2.0 × 1.6 mm L1.
  • Strict paste-free EP thermal vias & no-POFV discipline: .kicad_dru forbids via holes in all 797 SMD signal pads and enforces 16 paste-aperture keepouts (4 split paste quadrants × 4 EPs) so all 17 EP GND thermal vias (5 on U3.61, 2 on U5.17, 5 on U1.41, 5 on U2.41) sit strictly inside the paste-free cross channels (0.000 mm² B.Paste overlap).
  • Test-pad probing density: 14 × Ø1.0 mm bare B.Cu test pads (0.75 mm courtyard radius, ≥ 1.8 mm centre-to-centre spacing where grouped) placed in open B.Cu pockets for handheld needle/tweezers probing during bench bring-up (or pogo-pin fixture prior to battery bonding).

8. Thermal management & heat-source budget

  • Heat sources & paths (17 paste-free EP thermal vias into 0.2104 mm-adjacent In2.Cu solid GND plane):
  • Battery discharge (0–35 °C ambient): 110 front LEDs + U1/U2 (5+5 EP vias) + U3 (5 EP vias) + U6 dissipate ~0.68 W total at GCC ≤ 105 (~146 mA LED avg, ~189 mA VSYS). At R_th ≈ 35 K/W, ΔT ≈ +23.8 K (~48.8 °C at 25 °C ambient); calibrated ADC4 throttles GCC linearly above 25 °C so board temperature stays < 55 °C (< 60 °C LiPo discharge limit, < 70 °C EVQP7J01P).
  • USB active charging (~{CHG} == 0 steady ≥ 1 s, 0–25 °C ambient — open — user sign-off) & post-charge USB: U5 (BQ24073, 2 paste-free EP vias, CE = GND, TS = 10 kΩ fixed, R4 = 3.0 kΩ → 297 mA typ / 328 mA max) dissipates 0.56 W typ (0.80–0.87 W max) during low-bat CC (5.0 V → 3.2 V), giving P_board ≈ 0.70–0.75 W typ even with LEDs throttled to GCC ≤ 9 (0 at low-bat CC, ≤ 24 CV). Charging is specified at ≤ 25 °C ambient (≤ 45 °C across the bulk 3.7–4.2 V curve; swapping R4 to 4.7 kΩ 189 mA cuts P_U5,typ to 0.36 W). Once ~{CHG} = Hi-Z on USB (~{PGOOD} == 0, VO(REG) = 4.4 V, P_U5 ≈ 0.10–0.12 W), static full-white caps at GCC ≤ 85 (~0.79 W total; ADC4 < 55 °C loop stays active and 300 mA bursts are disabled on USB).

11. Keepouts and rule areas (hw/sunflower/s2-110-usbc-s3d/sunflower.kicad_pcb)

  1. Front LED & NPTH keepouts (J1 NPTH W, J1 NPTH E, Q2 optical keepout on F.Cu): 2 r = 0.525 mm (0.325 mm drill radius + 0.200 mm copper clearance) copper/via keepout discs around J1's Ø0.65 mm NPTH holes (±2.89, +13.00) enforced by .kicad_dru "J1 NPTH F.Cu copper keepout" (disallow pad track via excluding J1's own mechanical NPTH pad), plus r = 1.45 mm courtyard-free optical rule area around Q2 (0.00, −18.20) enforced by .kicad_dru "Q2 optical F.Cu LED keepout" (disallow footprint, corresponding to the 2.50 mm center-to-center LED seed exclusion where nearest LED D65 sits at 2.657 mm center / 1.505 mm courtyard / 1.701 mm pad).
  2. LX cutout L3 (In2.Cu) & LX inner guard (In1.Cu): Cuts the adjacent In2.Cu GND plane directly beneath U3.48/L1 and forbids ANALOG class nets plus +3V3/VSYS/VBAT/VBUS/VREG_AVDD/LIGHT/VBAT_SENSE copper/zones on In1.Cu above LX.
  3. L1 window (B.Cu) & CFILT PGND sep (B.Cu): Keeps B.Cu under L1 clean and isolates C17.2's dedicated GND via from PGND's 2 vias.
  4. U3 ring & U3 band: Protect the 1-piece +1V1 RP2350 ring and 1-piece +3V3 RP2350 band from foreign tracks/vias.
  5. U1/U2/U3/U5 solid EP + 16 paste keepout areas: Enforce GND-only thermal vias strictly inside the paste-free cross corridors (0.000 mm² B.Paste overlap).
  6. BT1 restricted & BT1 pad via guard (Molex 532610001-SD Sh. 2, B.Cu): BT1 restricted forbids B.Cu surface tracks/pours under Molex's 3.0 × 2.5 mm contact-beam housing window AND blocks all standard through-vias (F.Cu–B.Cu) through that window (inner In1.Cu/In2.Cu copper is buried under FR-4 + B.Mask and cannot contact Molex B.Cu beams); BT1 pad via guard keeps vias ≥ 0.35 mm clear of BT1.1/BT1.2.
  7. Rear Ø35.0 mm 403535 battery stack (open — user sign-off): Because Ø35.0 mm overlaps J1 (3.26 ± 0.15 mm) and BT1 (3.20–3.40 mm) in XY (21.55 mm chord), requires 0.05 mm Kapton tape over B.Cu test pads + either a ≥ 3.50 mm pocketed foam / 3D-printed carrier standoff (~10.0 mm total bare stack) or a smaller ≤ 30 mm cell on 2.4 mm foam inside the central component pocket.
