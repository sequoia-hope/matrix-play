Bench Bring-Up & Smoke-Test Plan — Sunflower s2-110-usbc (Rev S3C)

Board: sunflower.kicad_pcb (Ø38.0 mm, 4-layer, 110× XL-1615RGBC-RF + 2× AW20198QNR + RP2350A). Probe map: bringup-map-bottom.png and assembly-bottom.png.

[!IMPORTANT] Perform all Steps 1–7 BEFORE bonding a 403535 LiPo pouch to B.Cu. Thirteen of the 14 bare Ø1.0 mm test pads lie inside the Ø35 mm battery envelope; once bring-up passes, apply 0.05 mm Kapton tape over B.Cu exposed test pads (TP1..TP7, TP14..TP16) and provide a ≥ 2.4 mm insulating foam/carrier standoff so the pouch clears J1 (3.26 mm) and mated BT1 (3.40 mm).

1. Test-Pad & Probe Reference (B.Cu, Board-Centre (0, 0) Coordinates)

Note: TP9 and TP10 do not exist in this variant (14 test pads total: TP1..TP8, TP11..TP16). Coordinates (x, y) below are KiCad top-view (F.Cu) board-centre coordinates; when looking directly at the back (B.Cu) as in bringup-map-bottom.png, horizontal X is mirrored so KiCad NW (BT1, SW2, TP8) appears on the right and KiCad NE (SW1, TP14..TP16) appears on the left. Because TP8 (GND) sits on the NW/W rim, you may also clip/probe GND on J1's metal USB-C shield shell at the South rim ((0.0, +15.6 mm)).

2. Unpowered Visual & Ohmmeter Inspection (Step 1)

  1. Microscope visual check (B.Cu & F.Cu): - Confirm BT1 (NW rim) has its mating mouth facing inward (ESE toward U3) and signal pins on the NW rim: BT1 Pin 1 (VBAT +) is the more northerly/NE pad (-13.44, -11.61) and Pin 2 (BAT_N -) is the more southerly/SW pad (-13.87, -10.43) — verify zero solder bridge to the tented VBAT via 0.107 mm from BT1.2! - Confirm J1 USB-C mouth overhangs the South rim (+0.25–0.30 mm) with clean fillets on all 16 SMD pins and 0 solder wicking from the 4 PTH shell slots onto adjacent F.Cu LEDs (D10, D104, D110). Because sunflower-B_Paste.gbp omits paste on J1's 4 PTH shell slots (and F.Cu soldering is forbidden 0.18 mm from the LEDs), if J1's shell legs were left unsoldered by SMT-only PCBA, carefully tack the outer B.Cu edges of J1's 4 shell slots under a microscope with a fine tip (≤ 320 °C, < 2 s) before plugging in a USB-C cable. - Confirm U5 (BQ24073) sits level (no tilt/solder balling from the 2 non-POFV thermal vias inside U5.17). - Confirm L1 (3.3 µH) polarity mark faces West (+1V1 / C18 side, pad 1).
  2. Cold continuity & diode/resistance checks (DMM in low-voltage < 0.5 V resistance mode, probing Ø1.0 mm test pads & 0402 resistor pads only — never 0.40 mm-pitch QFN pins): - Verify no rail shorts between TP8/J1-shell (GND) and TP13 (VBUS), TP3 (VBAT), TP1 (VSYS), TP2 (+3V3), and TP4 (+1V1). - Verify BT1.1 ↔ BT1.2 resistance is > 1 MΩ (0 short across raw cell pins). - (Note: NT1 board-ID strap on GPIO19 sits under solder mask and connects directly to 0.40 mm-pitch U3.31; do not cold-probe U3.31 — NT1 is verified cleanly in firmware at Smoke-Test Step 1, ID = 6.) - Verify USB-C Rd pull-downs on J1: probe 0402 resistor pads R1 (5.1 kΩ ±1%) and R2 (5.1 kΩ ±1%) to GND.

3. Current-Limited USB-C First Power-Up (No Battery Attached, Steps 2–4)

  1. Step 2 — Current-limited 5.0 V apply (100 mA compliance via USB-C breakout): - Connect bench PSU 5.00 V / 100 mA current limit through a USB-C breakout cable to J1 (note U5 cold-starts in USB100 until +3V3 rises above 1.4 V on EN1). - Expected input current (blank flash / ROM USB boot): 12–28 mA (RP2350A USB boot + BQ24073 quiescent + AW20198 LED_SDB disabled via R31 = 100 kΩ pull-down). If compliance trips (> 60 mA), remove power immediately.
  2. Step 3 — Rail & clock verification: - TP13 (VBUS) = 4.90–5.05 V - TP1 (VSYS) = 4.30–4.50 V (4.40 V typ, BQ24073 regulated system output; ~{PGOOD} low on GPIO13) - TP2 (+3V3) = 3.250–3.350 V (< 10 mVpp AC ripple) - TP4 (+1V1) = 1.067–1.133 V (< 25 mVpp AC ripple) - Scope C24 (Y1 XOUT 15 pF cap pad 1 with 10× < 10 pF probe): 12.000 MHz sine/clipped wave (~0.8–1.8 Vpp).
  3. Step 4 — USB Full-Speed enumeration & SW2 (BOOTSEL) check: - Connect J1 to host PC USB port: verify lsusb enumerates 2e8a:000f Raspberry Pi RP2350 Boot (RPI-RP2 mass-storage drive). - After flashing test firmware, hold SW2 (NW rim BOOT, KiCad (-16.15, -6.20)) while pulsing TP5 (RUN) low → confirm clean re-entry to RP2350 Boot.

4. Firmware Smoke-Test Sequence & Mandatory Register Rules (Steps 5–6)

[!CAUTION] Four firmware initialization rules are mandatory on s2-110-usbc to prevent silent bus failure, ADC 37% error, or SW1/SW2/LiPo overheating: 1. GPIO4 / GPIO5 (I2C0) pad drive = 12 mA BEFORE first AW20198 write: At POR (LGC = 0), AW20198 requires VIL ≤ 0.40 V (1 kΩ pull-ups R21/R22 draw 3.3 mA, giving 0.43 V at 4 mA reset drive); set PADS_BANK0: GPIO4, GPIO5 DRIVE = 12mA (VOL ≈ 0.08–0.14 V) before starting I2C0. 2. AW20198 GCR (0x00, Page 0) = 0xB9 (NOT 0x81!): Bit 3 is LGC = 1 (3.3 V logic threshold: VIL ≤ 0.60 V, VIH ≥ 2.40 V, vs POR default LGC = 0 1.4 V / 0.4 V), bits [7:4] = 0b1011 (SWSEL) enable all 11 scan switches (SW1–SW11), bit 0 = CHIPEN = 1. (0x81 leaves LGC = 0 and sets SWSEL = 1000, disabling SW10–SW11!) 3. Set OD = 1, IE = 0, PDE = 0, PUE = 0 on GPIO26 (ADC0) and GPIO27 (ADC1) (rp2350.pdf p. 1068), clear PDE = 0, IE = 0 on unused floating GPIOs (GPIO0, 7..9, 14..18, 20..23, erratum RP2350-E9), and drive GPIO6 (LED_SDB) cleanly low/high (never tri-stated with IE = 1 across C35 = 100 nF): Wait ≥ 30 ms (6 τ for R11||R12 = 50 kΩ, C7 = 100 nF, τ = 5.0 ms) after asserting GPIO28 = 1 (VBAT_EN) before sampling ADC0, then set GPIO28 = 0. 4. Active thermal cap & U4 XIP mode on Rev S3C: Cap continuous full-white GCC ≤ 105 on pouch-backed battery (R_th ≈ 35 K/W → ≤ 48 °C skin / < 60 °C LiPo / < 70 °C EVQP7J01P) and GCC ≤ 9 (0 at low-bat CC, ≤ 24 in CV taper) whenever (GPIO13 ~{PGOOD} == 0) || (GPIO12 ~{CHG} == 0) (≤ 45 °C LiPo charge ceiling because BQ24073 TS is fixed by 10 kΩ R3 and ~{CHG} goes High-Z on recharge cycles!), plus cold-boot ADC4 ΔT ≤ +15 °C relative throttling. Because U4.8's nearest decoupling cap (C14) is 8.56 mm (4 vias) away on Rev S3C, configure RP2350 QMI XIP in 1-bit (0x0B) or 2-bit Dual-SPI (0xBB) at ≤ 50–66 MHz (SR3.DRV = 25%) to avoid 4-bit Quad-SPI read SSO droop (Relocate C22 to U4.8 in Rev S3D).

Smoke-Test Checklist

  1. Board ID (GPIO19..21 with internal pull-ups enabled): Read (GPIO21<<2)|(GPIO20<<1)|GPIO19 → expect 6 (0b110), then disable pull-ups and clear IE = 0 on GPIO20, 21.
  2. I2C1 (GPIO2 SDA / GPIO3 SCL, 400 kHz, R20/R19 = 4.7 kΩ) accelerometer (U9 LIS2DW12 at 0x19): Read WHO_AM_I (0x0F) → expect 0x44; verify 1 g gravity vector flips cleanly when turning board face-up vs face-down.
  3. I2C0 (GPIO4 SDA / GPIO5 SCL, 1 MHz, R21/R22 = 1 kΩ; set GPIO6 LED_SDB = 1, wait 2 ms): - Scan I2C0: confirm ACK at 0x20 (U1, West, AD1=GND, AD0=GND) and 0x25 (U2, East, AD1=VCC, AD0=VCC) (broadcast 0x5A). - Write Page 0 (0xF0 = 0xC0), GCR (0x00) = 0xB9, GCC (0x01) = 0x40 (25% safe bring-up brightness). - Walk single R, G, B channels across D1 (centre (0, 0)) through D110: confirm all 330 dies illuminate with 0 dead/swapped channels and verify uniform R/G/B colour.
  4. Rim button SW1 (GPIO11 via 1 kΩ R18, internal pull-up) & ambient light Q2 (GPIO1 → GPIO27/ADC1): - Press SW1 (NE rim): confirm GPIO11 transitions 1 → 0. - Set GPIO1 = 1, wait 15 ms (~6.8 τ for R43 = 22 kΩ, C70 = 100 nF), sample ADC1: confirm < 0.15 V covered in dark vs 1.0–2.9 V under desk lamp (saturates ~293–470 lx), then set GPIO1 = 0.

5. LiPo Battery Attach, XB9901A Wake & Charge Verification (Step 7)

  1. Verify plug polarity BEFORE mating to BT1: Confirm with DMM on the Molex 51021-0200 2-pin plug that + (red, 3.7–4.2 V) aligns with BT1 Pin 1 (North/NE pad) and - (black) aligns with BT1 Pin 2 (South/SW pad).
  2. First-attach XB9901A (U8) 0 V lockup wake: After mating the LiPo to BT1 with USB unplugged, TP3 (VBAT) may initially read 0.0 V because XB9901A enters power-down on first cell attach (xb9901a.pdf p. 7 Note 3). Plug in USB-C (J1) briefly (1–2 s): charger voltage across VBAT/GND wakes U8 out of shipping/lockup state, after which TP3 reads VBAT (3.7–4.2 V) continuously when USB is unplugged.
  3. Verify BQ24073 charge current & Q1 VBAT telemetry: - With a partially discharged (3.7–3.9 V) ≥ 300 mAh 403535 cell attached and USB-C at 5.0 V / 500 mA limit (LED_SDB = 0), verify GPIO13 (~{PGOOD}) = 0, GPIO12 (~{CHG}) = 0, and charge current into battery (R4 = 3.0 kΩ ISET) is 263–328 mA (297 mA typ), tapering cleanly at 4.20 V ±1%. - Trigger GPIO28 = 1, wait 30 ms, read ADC0: confirm 2 × V_ADC0 matches DMM voltage on TP3 within ±50 mV.
  4. Deep-sleep battery current check (intent R17, before applying Kapton & bonding LiPo): - Feed 3.70 V (50 mA limit) from a bench source / µA meter (e.g. Nordic PPK2 or DMM µA range) into BT1 (Pin 1 = +3.70 V, Pin 2 = -), wake U8 once via USB-C if needed, unplug USB-C, and command firmware into dormant/deep sleep (GPIO6 LED_SDB = 0, GPIO1 = 0, GPIO28 = 0, LIS2DW12 low-power wake mode, RP2350 P1.7 sleep with RP2350-E9 IE = 0 / latched pull-downs cleared): confirm steady-state sleep current is ≤ 85 µA typ (68–85 µA expected, ≤ 135 µA worst-case max).
