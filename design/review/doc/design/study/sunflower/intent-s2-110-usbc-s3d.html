Design intent: Sunflower s2-110-usbc-s3d (Rev S3D)

Phase 0 specification for hw/sunflower/s2-110-usbc-s3d/. Every item has a concrete number or an explicit "don't care". Parent design: intent-s2-110-usbc.md (Rev S3C), design/intent.md (Round Pendant), and README.md. Items marked (open — user sign-off) require user confirmation at Gate 0.

Purpose

Rev S3D respin of the low-cost 4-layer, 38.0 mm round wearable RGB LED display (s2-110-usbc), resolving all Gate 5–10 findings from Rev S3C while replacing the hybrid through-hole-shell USB-C receptacle with the full-SMT HRO TYPE-C-31-M-22 (C530612). Built exclusively from parts in local JLCPCB PCBA stock with standard non-filled through vias (no POFV). The front carries 110 XL-1615RGBC-RF RGB LEDs in a golden-angle Vogel spiral starting with D1 at (0, 0) plus a 12-o'clock top-rim ambient light sensor (Q2). All control, power, charging, motion sensing, bring-up test pads, and connectors sit on B.Cu, backed by a 35 mm round 403535 LiPo cell plugged into a right-angle Molex PicoBlade 1.25 mm socket (BT1). Charges and takes firmware over J1, runs animations from an RP2350A with full 4-bit Quad-SPI XIP (≤ 75 MHz), and sleeps at ≤ 85 µA typ (≤ 135 µA worst-case max in standby, or ≤ 95 µA with W25Q128JV 0xB9 deep power-down) until woken by a side button (SW1) or accelerometer motion (U9). Intended for personal use and small prototype runs.

Open source or confidential

Open source (CERN-OHL-P-2.0), public GitHub repository https://github.com/sequoia-hope/round-pendant, KiCad 9, Boardroom project layout. Front display carries no text; board revision/repo identity on back silkscreen is don't care where displaced by functional connector/test-pad labels (tracked via schematic/PCB title block and BOARD_ID = 4 hardware strap via NT1 on GPIO19 and NT2 on GPIO20; (open — user sign-off)).

Performance targets

  • System & QSPI clock: RP2350A 150 MHz max (48–150 MHz active), full 4-bit Quad-SPI (0xEB) XIP at up to 75 MHz (13.3 ns period with RXDELAY = 1..2, enabled by C22 placed 1.40 mm from U4.8).
  • Display refresh & PWM: ≥ 60 fps frame updates over 1 MHz I2C0; AW20198 62.5 kHz PWM / 5.05 kHz 11-row scan (SSCR.SSE = 1 spread-spectrum available).
  • ADC & thermal-sensor accuracy: VBAT (ADC0) ±5% uncalibrated (±1.5% after 1-point trim; clean ADC_AVDD reference with In2.Cu LX cutout L3 and isolated CFILT via); ADC4 internal temperature sensor uses a 1-point room-temperature trim (or -10 °C conservative trip margin to bound ±1.5% TPS7A0233 / dropout ADC_AVDD shift at -4.2 °C/%); LIGHT (ADC1) monotonic 8-bit indoor dimming bucket (don't care on absolute lux accuracy).
  • Wake latency & runtime: < 50 ms button wake (SW1) and ≤ 80 ms motion wake (U9 at 12.5 Hz LP1, within < 100 ms don't care bound); ≥ 2 h typical animated runtime (~160–189 mA avg) on 400 mAh cell, ≥ 4 months shelf sleep at ~72 µA.

Environment

  • Temperature: 0–35 °C discharge operating ambient; 0–25 °C USB battery-charging ambient with R4 = 3.0 kΩ (0–30 °C if user selects R4 = 4.7 kΩ; -20 to +60 °C storage without LiPo). Moisture/vibration/enclosure airflow: don't care (bare/open prototype in still air).
  • ESD: ±8 kV contact on J1 USB-C via inline U7 (USBLC6-2P6) + 27 Ω R14/R15; buttons and back test pads: don't care beyond 1 kΩ series limiting and bench handling.
  • Thermal:
  • Battery discharge (0–35 °C ambient): At 25 °C ambient, GCC ≤ 105 (~189 mA VSYS, ~0.68 W total) at R_th ≈ 35 K/W gives ΔT ≈ +23.8 K (~48.8 °C board); as ambient rises toward 35 °C, closed-loop calibrated ADC4 throttles GCC linearly so the board stays < 55 °C (< 60 °C LiPo discharge limit, < 70 °C EVQP7J01P, < 85 °C TYPE-C-31-M-22 / ICs).
  • USB active charging (~{CHG} == 0 steady ≥ 1 s, 0–25 °C ambient) & post-charge USB (~{PGOOD} == 0, ~{CHG} = Hi-Z): With U5 (BQ24073) CE = GND and TS = 10 kΩ fixed, U5 linearly drops VBUS − VBAT (P_U5 = 0.56 W typ at 5.0 V → 3.2 V, 297 mA, P_board ≈ 0.70–0.75 W typ with LEDs at GCC = 0..9, 0.99 W worst-case corner; 2 paste-free EP vias into In2.Cu GND). At ≤ 25 °C indoor charging ambient, nominal CC peak stays ~49–51 °C briefly near 3.2 V and drops ≤ 45 °C across the bulk 3.7–4.2 V charge curve (P_board ≤ 0.55 W); selecting R4 = 4.7 kΩ (189 mA, Open Question #2) lowers P_U5,typ to 0.36 W (ΔT ≈ +16 K, ≤ 41 °C at 25 °C). Once charging finishes (~{CHG} = Hi-Z, ~{PGOOD} == 0), linear drop from 5.0 V VBUS (VO(REG) = 4.4 V, P_U5 ≈ 0.10–0.12 W) caps static full-white at GCC ≤ 85 (~0.79 W total; ~0.93 W if GCC = 105), governed continuously by closed-loop ADC4 (< 55 °C) with 300 mA bursts disabled on USB so VBAT never dips into silent Hi-Z refresh charge (bq24075.pdf p. 25).

Mechanical

  • Outline & mounting: 38.0 mm circle, 0 mounting holes; held by rim/bezel outside r > 18.5 mm in a future housing (don't care for this prototype revision; bezel notches J1 (capturing its gull-wing SMD shell against cable peel), SW1, SW2, BT1 rear solder tips (r = 18.59 mm), and leaves an optical window at Q2).
  • Heights: Front ≤ 0.95 mm (Q2 0.80 ± 0.15 mm, D1–D110 0.60 ± 0.25 mm); PCB 1.60 ± 0.16 mm; Back components ≤ 3.41 mm (BT1 3.20 +0.20/−0.05 mm unmated / 3.40 mm mated; J1 TYPE-C-31-M-22 3.26 ± 0.15 mm height, 0.10 mm tab coplanarity; J1's two Ø0.50 mm × 0.70 ± 0.05 mm locating pegs leave 0.85–0.95 mm nominal / 0.69 mm worst-case FR-4 below F.Cu with 0.00 mm front protrusion).
  • Battery stack & harness: 35 mm × 4.0 mm 403535 cell sits behind B.Cu. BT1 (−11.40, −10.20 mm, −110°) faces inward toward U3 (1.0 mm mated plug protrusion, 3.02 mm unmated mouth clearance to C9, 4.04 mm to U3 body); the 1.25 mm plug is polarity-verified and inserted before bonding the cell over 0.05 mm Kapton tape + rear foam/carrier standoff. Geometry note for Gate 0 sign-off: because a Ø35.0 mm cell overlaps J1 (3.26 ± 0.15 mm) and BT1 (3.20–3.40 mm) in XY (21.55 mm chord between them), a flat 2.4 mm foam disc would interfere by 0.81–0.96 mm — the standoff must either be ≥ 3.50 mm thick (~10.0 mm total bare stack) with connector/harness relief cutouts, or a smaller cell (≤ 30 mm chord) must be used inside the 2.4 mm central pocket (open — user sign-off).
  • Connector overhangs: J1 (TYPE-C-31-M-22 at (0, +14.17 mm, 180°)) mouth edge reaches y = +19.290 mm (+0.290 mm overhang past R = 19.00 mm on axis; +0.823 mm axial / +0.801 mm radial at x = ±4.47 mm mouth corners); SW1/SW2 plungers overhang NE/NW rim by +0.40 mm nominal unpressed (+0.20 mm pressed; 0.00 to −0.20 mm worst-case tolerance — open — user sign-off).

Manufacturing

  • Fab & PCBA: JLCPCB 4-layer standard FR-4 (JLC04161H-7628, 1.6 mm, ENIG 1U" recorded directly in .kicad_pcb, green LPI solder mask, tented vias), 100% surface-mount double-sided PCBA (0 PTH solder joints or hand-tack steps!).
