Mechanical and Layout Brief — Round Pendant Variant f-usbc

Parent board: Variant F (hw/round-pendant/f/round-pendant.kicad_pcb, brief design/brief.md, stackup design/stackup.md). Variant folder: hw/round-pendant/f-usbc/.

This document records every Phase 4 mechanical and layout brief item (intent-and-brief.md) for round-pendant-f-usbc, where J1 (round_pendant:USB_Pads wire pads on Variant F) is replaced by the full-SMT 16-pin USB-C receptacle Korean Hroparts Elec TYPE-C-31-M-22 (C530612) via a local rip-up and re-route of the southern B.Cu I/O sector while keeping all 235 front LEDs (D1..D235), Q2, SW1, SW2, U1..U6, U9..U12, and all 147 locked RP2350 decoupling tracks 100% untouched.

1. Board Outline & Size

  • Outline: Circle, Ø38.000 mm (center = (100.000, 100.000) mm, radius = 19.000 mm), unchanged from Variant F (brief.md §1).
  • Copper-to-edge rule: 0.200 mm minimum (round-pendant.kicad_pro).
  • On J1 ((107.787, 110.926, -156.05°)), the four S1 SMD shell pad centers sit at (113.330, 110.915), (111.747, 107.351), (104.063, 115.031), and (102.480, 111.467) (max pad-center radius = 17.283 mm).
  • The outermost actual copper pad corner (East front S1 2.30 × 1.80 mm roundrect, rratio = 0.12, (114.746, 111.271) mm) sits at r = 18.560 mm unrounded (~18.47 mm rounded) — 0.440–0.530 mm inside Edge.Cuts (> 2.2× the 0.200 mm DRC floor).
  • On BT1 ((100.000, 117.100, 180°)), the two 1.60 × 2.40 mm wire pads reach r_outer = 18.366 mm (0.634 mm inside Edge.Cuts).

8. Thermal Management & Wearable Power Envelope

  • Whole-board thermal resistance (si_audit.py §5): On a Ø38.0 mm disc (11.34 cm² per face), natural convection + ε = 0.9 solder-mask radiation yields θ_BA ≈ 32.4 °C/W in free air (both faces exposed) and θ_BA ≈ 49.5 °C/W when worn against clothing/skin with the rear LiPo insulating ~70% of B.Cu.
  • Continuous vs burst LED power:
  • Continuous wearable ceiling: ≤ 0.51 W total (~100 mA total LED average current, GCC ≤ 0x55), limiting steady-state worn board rise to ΔT ≤ 22–25 °C (T_board ≤ 47–50 °C at 25 °C ambient, within the 50 °C wearable skin limit).
  • Peak 1.26 W (300 mA LED total): Short-duration animation bursts (≤ 15 s) or free-air bench demo only (ΔT_free ≈ 40.8 °C, ΔT_worn ≈ 62.3 °C if sustained continuously).
  • USB charging (BQ24073 297 mA, 0.48–0.79 W peak at 3.0 V cell): Firmware must blank or cap LEDs to < 5 mA whenever ~CHG == 0 (GPIO12, U3 Pin 16) so charger + LED power never exceeds 0.55 W (T_cell ≤ 42 °C < 45 °C LiPo charge limit), backed by closed-loop RP2350 ADC4 die-temperature foldback (bringup-f-usbc.md §4 Step 3.3).
