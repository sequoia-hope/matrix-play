Bench Bring-Up & Smoke-Test Plan — Round Pendant Variant f-usbc-rtc (Rev FUR)

Board: round-pendant.kicad_pcb (Ø38.0 mm, 8-layer 1.0 mm ENIG POFV, 235× XL-1010RGB-YG + 5× IS31FL3745-CLS4-TR WLCSP-36 + RP2350A + BQ24073 + TYPE-C-31-M-22 USB-C + inward A1251WR-S-2P 1.25 mm battery socket BT1 + RX8130CE:BB RTC U13). Assembly drawings & renders: assembly-top.pdf, assembly-bottom.pdf, schematic.pdf.

[!IMPORTANT] 1. Complete Steps 1–3.5 on USB-C BEFORE plugging a 1.25 mm LiPo battery into BT1 (CJT A1251WR-S-2P, C225111). 2. MANDATORY 1.25 mm LiPo Plug Polarity Verification: Commercial 2-pin 1.25 mm (MX1.25 / PicoBlade) LiPo packs ship in both Red/Black orientations (~50/50 across vendors). Although U8 (XB9901A, xb9901a.pdf p. 1) blocks reverse battery voltage when USB is unplugged, R8 (100 Ω 0402, 62.5 mW rated) would dissipate ~122 mW continuously under reverse hookup (and connecting USB while reversed stresses U8/U5). Before mating the plug into BT1, hold the plug aligned with BT1's NNE mouth and verify with a DMM that the Red (+) wire aligns with BT1.1 (VBAT, marked + on B.SilkS, NW rear pin at (87.521, 112.376)) and the Black (-) wire aligns with BT1.2 (BAT_N, marked - on B.SilkS, SE rear pin at (88.625, 112.962)). If reversed, gently lift the two plastic crimp-housing lances with a needle/tweezers and swap the two contacts in 10 s before plugging in! 3. Rear LiPo Mechanical Stackup & Ultrasonic Prohibition: NEVER ultrasonic-clean the assembled PCB (U13 RX8130CE:BB built-in 32.768 kHz tuning-fork crystal can fracture per ETM50E-10 §12.2.(3) p. 20!). Insulate TP1..TP8 with 0.05 mm Kapton tape, mate the 1.25 mm plug into BT1 before adhering the rear battery (4.88 mm flat runway to 0.80 mm-tall U4), and mount the 403535 round LiPo pouch (35 mm round × 4.0 mm thick, ~400–450 mAh) on a 3.3–3.5 mm foam standoff flush with J1 (3.26 mm) and BT1 (3.20 mm).

2. Step 1 — Unpowered Visual & Cold DMM Check

  1. Optical / microscope inspection: - J1 (TYPE-C-31-M-22, SSE rim): Confirm all 4 S1 gull-wing SMD shell tabs (2.30 × 1.80 mm) and 12 rear signal fingers have uniform wetting with zero bridges, and confirm 2× Ø0.65 mm NPTH holes on F.Cu ({D234, D235, D231, D226} and {D76, D66, D74, D64}) show zero peg protrusion. - BT1 (CJT A1251WR-S-2P, SW (89.200, 110.550, -28°)): Confirm both 2.10 × 3.00 mm MP mechanical tabs (GND) and both rear 0.80 × 1.60 mm signal leads (BT1.1 VBAT, BT1.2 BAT_N) are cleanly reflowed at CPL = 208.00°, mouth facing NNE (+28° E of N) toward U4 (4.88 mm nominal / 4.65 mm min flat plug runway to U4, 5.85 mm to C22). - U13 (RX8130CE:BB, East (114.750, 101.200, 0°)): Confirm Pin-1 chamfer (SW corner (113.65, 102.75)) matches the B.SilkS OD = 0.30 mm pin-1 dot and all 10 ceramic castellated/bottom lands wet cleanly (C75 100 nF at 1.23 mm east of Pin 8, C76 1 µF at 1.23 mm east of Pin 9). - U1, U2, U10, U11, U12 (IS31FL3745 WLCSP-36) & Q2 (ALS-PT19): Confirm A1 / collector alignment against pin-1 dots.
  2. Cold DMM resistance checks (< 0.5 V ohmmeter, probing test/passive pads only): - Rail-to-GND (TP8): confirm > 10 kΩ steady-state on C1.1 (VBUS), TP1 (VSYS), TP3 (VBAT), TP2 (+3V3), TP4 (+1V1), and C76.1 (RTC_VOUT). - BT1: confirm BT1.1 (+) ↔ BT1.2 (-) reads > 1 MΩ, BT1.MP ↔ TP8 reads < 0.1 Ω, and unpowered BT1.2 (-) ↔ TP8 reads open (> 100 kΩ) before U8 wake. - USB-C Rd: probe R1 ((102.50, 116.24)) and R2 ((104.51, 117.00)) → confirm 5.1 kΩ ±1% each to GND.

3. Step 2 — Current-Limited USB-C Power-Up & Enumeration (No Battery)

  1. Set bench supply to 5.00 V, 100 mA current limit into J1 (test both USB-C orientations).
  2. Expected quiescent current (blank flash / ROM boot, LED_SDB low): 14–30 mA.
  3. Verify rails: C1.1 (VBUS) = 4.90–5.05 V, TP1 (VSYS) = 4.30–4.50 V, TP2 (+3V3) = 3.250–3.350 V, TP4 (+1V1) = 1.067–1.133 V, R32.2 (RTC_INT) = 3.25–3.35 V.
  4. USB 2.0 Full-Speed enumeration (12 Mbit/s): Confirm 2e8a:000f Raspberry Pi RP2350 Boot (RPI-RP2) enumerates in both USB-C plug orientations; verify SW2 (BOOTSEL) + TP5 (RUN) UF2 re-entry.

4. Step 3 — Firmware Smoke Test (RP2350, RX8130CE RTC, LIS2DW12 & 235 LEDs)

  1. Mandatory RP2350A pad setup (rp2350-core.md): - Clear default pad pull-downs (PDE = 0, PUE = 0 in PADS_BANK0) on all externally pulled-up pins (GPIO2, GPIO3, GPIO4, GPIO5, GPIO11, GPIO12, GPIO13, GPIO19 — I2C1, I2C0, SW1, ~CHG, ~PGOOD, RTC_INT!). Leaving PDE = 1 on GPIO19 creates a 100 kΩ / 50 kΩ divider (~1.1 V phantom low + 22 µA leak)! - Set GPIO4, GPIO5 (I2C0) drive strength to 12 mA (1 kΩ pull-ups R21/R22). - Set OD = 1, IE = 0, PDE = 0, PUE = 0 on GPIO26/ADC0 (VBAT_SENSE) and GPIO27/ADC1 (LIGHT), and IE = 0, PDE = 0 on unused GPIOs (RP2350-E9).
  2. I2C1 (GPIO2 SDA / GPIO3 SCL, 400 kHz max) — U9 (LIS2DW12 @ 0x19) & NEW U13 (RX8130CE:BB @ 0x32): - Scan I2C1 at 100–400 kHz: confirm ACK from 0x19 (U9, WHO_AM_I 0x0F == 0x44) and 0x32 (U13 RX8130CE:BB, rtc-rx8130ce.md). - Mandatory RX8130CE:BB initialization sequence (ETM50E-10 §10.1 Fig. 14 p. 17, §18.1–18.3 pp. 55–57):
    1. Wait > 30 ms (t_CL) after +3V3 POR, issue 1 dummy I2C read of 1Dh (ignore ACK/NACK to clear any half-clocked bus state), then read Flag Register 1Dh (note ETM50E-10 Table 7 p. 15's 0.95 s I2C bus timeout).
    2. If VLF (1Dh bit 1) == 1 (cold power-up / crystal starting): note that 30 ms (t_CL) is only the wait before reading VLF, whereas crystal start-up is t_str = 0.19 s typ, 1.0 s max (Table 4 p. 12, Fig. 42 p. 55: "VLF cannot be cleared to 0 until internal oscillation starts"). (If +3V3 could have been at 0 V for < 10 s, first write the §18.2 Software Reset sequence p. 56.) Poll writing 1Dh = 0x00 every 25–50 ms (up to 1.2 s timeout) until reading 1Dh returns VLF == 0.
    3. Pulse INIEN once and lock backup charger off (ETM50E-10 §15.5 Table 35 Note 1 p. 42 & §18.3 Fig. 44 p. 57): write 1Fh = 0x10 (INIEN = 1, CHGEN = 0), then write 1Fh = 0x00 (INIEN = 0, CHGEN = 0 — mandatory when VBAT Pin 10 is tied to +3V3 per §4.2 EX7!), and write 30h = 0x00 (clear Digital Offset).
    4. Write Extension Register 1Ch = 0xC0 (FSEL1:0 = 11b in bits [7:6], TE = 0 in bit 4): disables the POR-default 32.768 kHz FOUT output driver, dropping U13 active current from 3.5 µA to 1.50 µA typ!
    5. Set STOP = 1 (1Eh = 0x40), write BCD calendar 10h..16h (e.g., 2026-11-09 18:30:00), clear STOP = 0 (1Eh = 0x00), wait 2.000 s, and read back 10h: confirm seconds incremented by +2! - Hardware RTC Wake (POWMAN PWRUP2 on GPIO19, U3 Pin 31 — ETM50E-10 §13.2 Table 12 p. 22 & §18.7 Fig. 48 p. 59):
    6. Stop timer first by writing 1Ch = 0xC2 (FSEL = 11b [FOUT = OFF], TE = 0 [bit 4], TSEL = 010b [1 Hz source clock]).
    7. Clear TF = 0 (1Dh = 0x00) and enable timer interrupt by writing 1Eh = 0x10 (TIE = 1, STOP = 0, TEST = 0, TSTP = 0, TBKE = 0).
    8. Load the 16-bit wakeup counter (1Ah = LSB weights 1..128, 1Bh = MSB weights 256..32768; both reset to undefined X!): write 1Ah = 0x02 and 1Bh = 0x00 (2 ticks @ 1 Hz -> 1.0–2.0 s first period per Fig. 20 p. 29).
    9. Arm RP2350 POWMAN PWRUP2 (SOURCE = 19, Offset 0x94) for active-low wake on GPIO19 (U3 Pin 31), then start the countdown by writing 1Ch = 0xD2 (TE = 1 in bit 4!).
    10. Put RP2350 into POWMAN dormant/off (P1.4 / P1.7): confirm U13.~IRQ (Pin 6) pulls RTC_INT (R32.2 / GPIO19) to 0.0 V (t_RTN2 = 7.57 ms min auto-release pulse, Table 16 p. 28) within 1.0–2.0 s and wakes RP2350 cleanly; read 1Dh to confirm TF == 1, then write 1Ch = 0xC0 (TE = 0) and 1Dh = 0x00 (TF = 0)!
  3. I2C0 (1 MHz) 5× IS31FL3745 (0x20, 0x22, 0x25, 0x26, 0x2F) & 235 LEDs (D1..D235): - Set GPIO6 (LED_SDB) = 1, set LGC = 1, SWS = 0011, PHC = 1 on U2/U11, GCC ≤ 0x40 (≤ 0.51 W continuous wearable ceiling; blank LEDs while ~CHG == 0; ADC4 foldback at 42 °C charge / 47 °C wear), and verify all 705 R/G/B dies light cleanly.
  4. Rim button SW1 (GPIO11) & ambient light sensor Q2 (GPIO1 → ADC1): Verify SW1 active-low press and Q2 dark vs lamp response (≥ 10 ms settle).

5. Step 4 — BT1 LiPo Plug-In, XB9901A Wake, Charge & Dormant + RTC Wake Current

  1. Dormant + RTC timed-wake current check (< 250 µA budget): - Feed 3.80 V (50 mA limit) via a 1.25 mm test pigtail into BT1 (Pin 1 = +3.80 V, Pin 2 = Return via µA meter), wake U8 with a 1 s 5 V USB-C pulse at J1, remove USB-C, confirm PDE = 0 on GPIO2–5, 11–13, 19, U13 FSEL = 11b (FOUT = OFF), LED_SDB = 0, LIGHT_PWR = 0, VBAT_EN = 0 (GPIO14), and enter RP2350 dormant with U13 wakeup timer running: confirm total battery current is ~20–205 µA (< 250 µA budget), and confirm U13 wakes the pendant cleanly on schedule!
  2. 403535 LiPo plug-in & BQ24073 charge verification (R4 = 3.0 kΩ → 297 mA typ): - Verify 403535 cell C-rate (R4 = 3.0 kΩ = 297 mA; swap R4 to 4.3–4.7 kΩ if cell requires ≤ 0.5C) and verify plug Red (+) = BT1.1 (+) / Black (-) = BT1.2 (-) with a DMM before mating! - Mate plug straight along the SSW axis (28° W of S) into BT1's NNE mouth, wake U8 via USB-C (5.0 V / 500 mA limit, LED_SDB = 0), verify ~PGOOD = 0, ~CHG = 0, 266–325 mA CC charge, 4.20 V ±1% CV termination, and GPIO14 = 1 (VBAT_EN, ≥ 30 ms wait) ADC0 (VBAT_SENSE) 2 × V_ADC0 == V_TP3 ± 50 mV.
