Bench Bring-Up & Smoke-Test Plan — Round Pendant Variant f-usbc

Board: round-pendant.kicad_pcb (Ø38.0 mm, 8-layer 1.0 mm ENIG POFV, 235× XL-1010RGB-YG + 5× IS31FL3745-CLS4-TR WLCSP-36 + RP2350A + BQ24073 + TYPE-C-31-M-22 USB-C). Assembly drawings & renders: assembly-top.pdf, assembly-bottom.pdf, schematic.pdf.

[!IMPORTANT] Complete Steps 1–4.1 BEFORE soldering a LiPo battery to BT1 (round_pendant:BatteryPads). All 8 bare Ø1.0 mm test pads (TP1..TP8) sit on B.Cu inside the Ø35 mm rear battery footprint. After bring-up passes, mask U8 temporarily with a strip of Kapton tape while hand-soldering BT1, verify BT1.2 (-) ↔ GND is not bridged (U8 XB9901A U8.1..3 sits 0.300 mm CAD / ~0.20 mm LPI dam west of BT1.2), insulate TP1..TP8 and BT1 joints with 0.05 mm Kapton tape, and mount the 403535 round LiPo pouch (35 mm round × 4.0 mm thick, ~400–450 mAh) on a 3.3–3.5 mm foam standoff matching J1's 3.16–3.26 mm shell height (leaving (105.8..110.0, 115.6..117.8) ahead of J1's mouth clear of tape/foam).

2. Step 1 — Unpowered Visual & Ohmmeter Check

  1. Optical / microscope inspection: - J1 (TYPE-C-31-M-22, SE rim): Confirm all 4 gull-wing SMD shell tabs (S1, 2.30 × 1.80 mm) and all 12 rear signal/power fingers have uniform wetting with zero solder bridges across the 0.50 mm-pitch center pins (B8, A5, B7, A6, A7, B6, A8, B5). On F.Cu, verify the two Ø0.65 mm NPTH holes sit cleanly in the mask web between {D234, D235, D231, D226} (NPTH #1 at (109.953, 108.684)) and {D76, D66, D74, D64} (NPTH #2 at (104.671, 111.030)) with zero peg protrusion (0.15–0.45 mm recessed below F.Cu). - U1, U2, U10, U11, U12 (IS31FL3745 WLCSP-36): Confirm coplanar seating and A1 alignment against the 5 B.SilkS OD = 0.30 mm A1 dots outside each courtyard. - Q2 (ALS-PT19, 12-o'clock F.Cu rim): Confirm collector mark aligns with Pad 1 (West, (97.214, 82.038)) matching the F.SilkS pin-1 dot.
  2. Cold DMM resistance checks (< 0.5 V ohmmeter mode, probing TP1..TP8, BT1, C1, R1, R2 only — never 0.4 mm QFN or 0.5 mm WLCSP balls): - Rail-to-GND (TP8 or J1 shell): confirm no shorts (> 10 kΩ steady-state after MLCC charge) on C1.1 (VBUS), TP1 (VSYS), TP3 (VBAT), TP2 (+3V3), and TP4 (+1V1). - Battery pads: confirm BT1.1 (+) ↔ BT1.2 (-) reads > 1 MΩ, and confirm unpowered BT1.2 (-) ↔ TP8 (GND) reads open (> 100 kΩ) prior to U8 wake (verifying no solder bridge across the 0.300 mm CAD / ~0.20 mm LPI B.Mask dam to U8.1..3). - USB-C sink Rd resistors: probe 0402 resistors R1 ((93.90, 115.57)) and R2 ((91.75, 115.49)) → confirm 5.1 kΩ ±1% each to GND.

3. Step 2 — Current-Limited USB-C First Power-Up (No Battery)

  1. Set a bench supply to 5.00 V, 100 mA current limit (BQ24073 starts in USB100 until +3V3 > 1.4 V raises EN1 into USB500, charger.md) and feed J1 via a USB-C breakout cable (test both plug orientations!).
  2. Expected quiescent input current (blank flash / ROM boot, LED_SDB pulled low by R31 = 100 kΩ): 14–30 mA. Cut power immediately if current hits compliance (> 60 mA).
  3. Measure every rail against datasheet limits: - C1.1 (VBUS): 4.90–5.05 V - TP1 (VSYS): 4.30–4.50 V (4.40 V typ, BQ24073 VO(REG)) - TP2 (+3V3): 3.250–3.350 V (TPS7A0233PDQNR ±1.5%, < 10 mVpp ripple) - TP4 (+1V1): 1.067–1.133 V (RP2350A core buck 1.10 V ±3%, < 25 mVpp ripple)
  4. USB 2.0 Full-Speed enumeration (12 Mbit/s) in both USB-C orientations: - Plug J1 into a host PC with a standard USB-C cable (flip plug 180° and repeat): confirm lsusb enumerates 2e8a:000f Raspberry Pi RP2350 Boot (RPI-RP2 UF2 drive) in both orientations (proving R1 CC1, R2 CC2, A6/B6 DP1/DP2, A7/B7 DN1/DN2, U7 USBLC6-2P6, and R14/R15 (27 Ω) series termination resistors). - Hold SW2 (NW rim BOOTSEL) while pulsing TP5 (RUN) to GND → confirm clean UF2 bootloader re-entry.

4. Step 3 — Firmware Smoke Test & Mandatory IS31FL3745 / RP2350 Init

  1. RP2350A errata & pad setup (rp2350-core.md): - Clear RP2350 default pad pull-downs (PDE = 0 in PADS_BANK0) on all externally pulled-up pins (GPIO2, GPIO3, GPIO4, GPIO5, GPIO11, GPIO12, GPIO13 — I2C1, I2C0, SW1, ~CHG, ~PGOOD) immediately on boot; leaving PDE = 1 burns ~50–150 µA across the external pull-ups and breaks the ≤ 85 µA sleep budget. - Set PADS_BANK0: GPIO4 (SDA), GPIO5 (SCL) drive strength to 12 mA before enabling I2C0 (1 kΩ pull-ups R21/R22 draw 3.3 mA; IS31FL3745 POR LGC = 0 requires VIL ≤ 0.40 V, drivers-bga.md). - Set OD = 1, IE = 0, PDE = 0, PUE = 0 on analog inputs GPIO26/ADC0 (VBAT_SENSE) and GPIO27/ADC1 (LIGHT), and clear IE = 0, PDE = 0 on unused GPIOs (RP2350-E9).
  2. I2C1 (GPIO2 SDA / GPIO3 SCL, 400 kHz) accelerometer (U9 LIS2DW12TR at 0x19): - Read WHO_AM_I (0x0F) → expect 0x44; verify ±1 g Z-axis sign flip when inverting the pendant.
  3. I2C0 (GPIO4 SDA / GPIO5 SCL, 1 MHz) 5× IS31FL3745-CLS4-TR LED matrix (D1..D235): - Drive GPIO6 (LED_SDB) = 1, wait 2 ms, and scan I2C0: confirm ACK from all 5 drivers (drivers-bga.md p. 7):
    • U1 = 0x20 (ADDR2=GND, ADDR1=GND)
    • U2 = 0x22 (ADDR2=GND, ADDR1=SDA)
    • U10 = 0x25 (ADDR2=SCL, ADDR1=SCL)
    • U11 = 0x26 (ADDR2=SCL, ADDR1=SDA)
    • U12 = 0x2F (ADDR2=VSYS, ADDR1=VSYS)
    • Unlock command register (0xFD = 0xC5), select Page 2 (0xFE = 0x02), and write:
    • Configuration (0x00): set LGC = 1 (3.3 V I2C thresholds), SWS = 0011 (SW1..SW8, 1/8 scan duty), SSD = 1 (normal operation), and set PHC = 1 (180° phase shift) on U2 and U11 to halve VSYS ripple.
    • De-Ghosting (0x02) = 0x33 (2 kΩ off-time discharge only).
    • Global Current Control (0x01) = 0x40 (25% bring-up limit; cap continuous wearable dissipation to ≤ 0.50 W / ~100 mA average total LED current (300 mA / 1.26 W for short bursts only), blank LEDs except < 5 mA indicator when ~CHG == 0, and implement closed-loop RP2350 ADC4 thermal foldback starting at 42 °C while charging and 47 °C while discharging).
    • Step single R, G, and B dots across all 235 LEDs (D1..D235): verify 705/705 dies light with zero open/shorted channels, particularly in the re-routed southern sector (D63..D91, D190..D235).
  4. Rim button SW1 (GPIO11) & ambient light sensor Q2 (ALS-PT19, GPIO1 → GPIO27/ADC1): - Press SW1 (NE rim) → confirm GPIO11 (U3 Pin 15) reads 0. - Set GPIO1 = 1 (LIGHT_PWR, U3 Pin 3), wait ≥ 10 ms (4.5 τ for R43 = 22 kΩ, C70 = 100 nF, light-sensor.md), read ADC1: confirm < 0.05 V dark vs ~0.33 V @ 100 lx / ~2.9 V under bright lamp, then clear GPIO1 = 0.

5. Step 4 — Battery Attach, XB9901A First-Wake, Charge & Sleep Current

  1. Sleep-current check before soldering LiPo (intent.md ≤ 85 µA budget): - Connect a 3.80 V source (50 mA limit) with series µA meter (e.g. PPK2) to BT1.1 (+) (101.60, 117.10) and BT1.2 (-) (98.40, 117.10). Briefly attach 5 V USB-C at J1 (1 s) to wake U8 (XB9901A enters 0 V-charger lockup on initial cell attach per xb9901a.pdf p. 7 Note 3), remove USB-C, confirm PDE = 0 is cleared on GPIO2–5, 11, 12, 13, and enter deep sleep (LED_SDB = 0 [GPIO6], LIGHT_PWR = 0 [GPIO1], VBAT_EN = 0 [GPIO14, U3 Pin 18], U9 low-power wake, RP2350 dormant): confirm ≤ 85 µA (~18–55 µA expected).
  2. LiPo soldering & BQ24073 charge check (R4 = 3.0 kΩ → 297 mA typ; check 403535 C-rate): - Cell C-rate check before soldering: R4 = 3.0 kΩ (C25784) programs ICHG = 297 mA (266–325 mA, 0.66C–0.81C on a 400–450 mAh 403535 cell). Verify the procured 403535 cell datasheet permits ≥ 0.8C charge; if rated ≤ 0.5C, swap 0402 R4 ((99.20, 109.40)) to 4.3 kΩ (207 mA) or 4.7 kΩ (189 mA). - Temporarily mask U8 (X ≤ 97.3 mm) with a strip of Kapton tape while hand-soldering cell + (red) to BT1.1 (+) and cell - (black) to BT1.2 (-) (dress wire leads northward toward the board interior and avoid iron contact south of BT1 at Y > 118.3 mm, where CC1 passes 0.114 mm south under LPI soldermask), remove the temporary mask, inspect under magnification that no solder or wire strand bridges across the 0.300 mm CAD (~0.20 mm LPI) mask dam to U8.1..3 (and re-confirm BT1.2 (-) ↔ TP8 reads open before waking U8), wake U8 via USB-C (5.0 V / 500 mA limit, LED_SDB = 0), and verify ~PGOOD = 0 (GPIO13), ~CHG = 0 (GPIO12), charge current 266–325 mA (297 mA typ with 3.0 kΩ), and 4.20 V ±1% CV termination. - Set GPIO14 = 1 (VBAT_EN, U3 Pin 18), wait ≥ 30 ms (R9 = R10 = 100 kΩ, C7 = 100 nF), sample ADC0 (GPIO26, U3 Pin 40, VBAT_SENSE), and confirm 2 × V_ADC0 matches TP3 within ±50 mV, then clear GPIO14 = 0.
