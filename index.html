RP2350 Hardware Design-Space Study

Matrix Play

Exploring geometry and patterns in RGB LED matrix layouts alongside PCB stackup, IC packaging, and cost trade-offs — comparing ultra-dense 20 × 20 mm 0808 square arrays on 8-layer 25 × 50 mm boards (up to 64 LEDs/cm² with south-bay QFN electronics), low-cost 4-layer 25 × 70 mm skewed-parallelogram boards, low-cost 4-layer Ø 38 mm sunflower boards (66–132 LEDs from $10.61), and 8-layer Ø 38 mm phyllotaxis (“sunflower”) pendants.

Interactive Workspace → GitHub

Ultra-Dense Square Matrix Family — 20 × 20 mm 0808 RGB Window

25 × 50 mm 8-layer ENIG rectangle packing up to 16 × 16 = 256 MHPA0808RGBDT (0.80 × 0.80 mm) RGB LEDs at 1.27 mm (50 mil) pitch inside a dedicated north 20 × 20 mm window — with zero components underneath and all 4× AW20198QNR QFN-40 drivers, RP2350A, charger, IMU, and sensor isolated in the south bay.

8 Layers · 0808 RGB · 64.0 LEDs/cm² · $20.27–$21.42 BOM

Sunflower 4-Layer Family — Ø 38 mm Low-Cost Round

The parallelogram’s low-cost parts (XL-1615RGBC-RF LEDs, AW20198QNR QFN-40 drivers) laid out as a golden-angle Vogel spiral on a Ø 38 mm disc, with the light sensor at the centre and Variant D’s RP2350 block on the back. Swept from 66 to 132 LEDs: every tier routes to 0 opens on 4 layers, because a spiral has no LED columns lined up over the drivers’ exposed pads. The 88-LED tier also routes with no vias in pads (no filled-via surcharge).

4 Layers · QFN-40 EP · 66–132 LEDs · $10.61–$12.60 BOM

Skewed Parallelogram Family — 4-Layer Low-Cost Density Sweep

25 × 70 mm envelope sheared 17.75° around a standard 20 × 35 mm prismatic LiPo cell. Zero BGA, solid QFN-40 exposed ground pads (AW20198QNR), 100% locally stocked JLCPCB BOM, swept from 66 to 264 RGB LEDs to map the exact 4-layer routing knee.

4 Layers · QFN-40 EP · $10.61–$19.03 BOM

Round Pendant Family — Ø 38 mm Rectangular Grid & Sunflower Phyllotaxis

Ø 38 mm wearable pendant evolving from an orthogonal 2.0 mm rectangular LED array (Variant A) to a 235-LED golden-angle sunflower spiral with Voronoi seed-web silkscreen, round smartwatch LiPo cell, and QFN-60 vs WLCSP-36 BGA driver architectures.

6–8 Layers · WLCSP-36 / QFN-60 · 234–235 LEDs
