RP2350 Hardware Design-Space Study

Non-Rectangular LED Matrix Topologies

Exploring artistic, non-grid RGB LED matrix layouts alongside PCB stackup, IC packaging, and cost trade-offs — comparing dense 8-layer Ø 38 mm phyllotaxis (“sunflower”) pendants using WLCSP-36 BGA drivers against low-cost 4-layer 25 × 70 mm skewed-parallelogram boards using exposed-pad QFNs and commodity prismatic LiPo cells.

Interactive Workspace → GitHub

Skewed Parallelogram Family — 4-Layer Low-Cost Density Sweep

25 × 70 mm envelope sheared 17.75° around a standard 20 × 35 mm prismatic LiPo cell. Zero BGA, solid QFN-40 exposed ground pads (AW20198QNR), 100% locally stocked JLCPCB BOM, swept from 66 to 264 RGB LEDs to map the exact 4-layer routing knee.

4 Layers · QFN-40 EP · $10.61–$19.03 BOM

Sunflower Round Pendant Family — 8-Layer High-Density Phyllotaxis

Ø 38 mm wearable pendant packing 235 RGB LEDs on a golden-angle spiral (~2.0 mm pitch, 20.7 LEDs/cm²) with a Voronoi seed-web front silkscreen, custom round smartwatch cell, and WLCSP-36 BGA vs QFN-48 driver architectures.

6–8 Layers · WLCSP-36 / QFN-48 · 234–235 LEDs
